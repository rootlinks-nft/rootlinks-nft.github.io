Rules规则

Every rule, as the contracts enforce it全部规则，均由合约强制执行

Who can mint谁可以铸造

Only a Binance Agentic Wallet that owns a SPACE ID .ai Agent ID. Every mint passes agentId = uint256(keccak256(bytes(handle))) and the contract checks the caller owns it in the registry. Limits follow the Agent ID, not the wallet.只有拥有 SPACE ID .ai Agent ID 的 Binance Agentic Wallet。每次铸造都传入 agentId = uint256(keccak256(bytes(handle)))，合约会在注册表中检查调用者是否拥有它。额度跟随 Agent ID，而不是钱包。

Per Agent ID每个 Agent ID

1 free mint (Free, or Invite-free through an inviter) and 5 paid at 0.003 BNB. Each other Agent ID it invites adds +1 paid, up to +5 (10 paid). One paid mint at 0.0015 BNB on the mint that first binds an inviter.1 个免费铸造（普通免费，或通过邀请人的邀请免费）和 5 个付费（每个 0.003 BNB）。每邀请一个其他 Agent ID 增加 1 个付费额度，最多 +5（付费共 10 个）。首次绑定邀请人的那次铸造可以 0.0015 BNB 付费铸造 1 个。

Supply总量

7,777 trees: Free 2,200 · Invite 1,800 · Paid 3,377 · Reserve 400. Reserve trees are minted by the project before close for giveaways and partners, at 1× weight and with no paid quota.7,777 棵树：免费 2,200 · 邀请 1,800 · 付费 3,377 · 预留 400。预留树由项目方在铸造结束前铸造，用于赠送和合作方，权重 1×，不增加付费额度。

Invites邀请

Each tree can invite 2 Agent IDs; each invite adds a glowing root and a stage (max stage 3). An Agent ID binds to an inviter once. It can’t invite itself or the wallet holding the tree.每棵树可以邀请 2 个 Agent ID；每次邀请增加一条发光的根并升一个阶段（最高第 3 阶段）。一个 Agent ID 只能绑定一次邀请人，不能邀请自己，也不能邀请持有该树的钱包。

Window and close铸造窗口与结束

72 hours from openMint(). The window closes the moment paid sells out, or anyone calls close() after 72h.从 openMint() 起 72 小时。付费部分售罄即刻结束，否则 72 小时后任何人都可以调用 close() 结束。

Reveal开图

On sell-out the contract commits to a future block; anyone then calls reveal(), and that block hash shuffles all trees into Common 5,590 · Uncommon 1,555 · Rare 555 · Legendary 76 · World Tree 1. Rarity is unknown to everyone before close.售罄后合约锁定一个未来区块，随后任何人都可以调用 reveal()，由该区块哈希把所有树随机分为：普通 5,590 · 罕见 1,555 · 稀有 555 · 传说 76 · 世界之树 1。铸造结束前任何人都不知道稀有度。

Token launch代币上线

The ownerless Distributor’s permissionless finalize() pairs 200M tokens with 80% of the raised BNB on PancakeSwap V2 and burns the LP tokens. 20% of the BNB goes to the creator payout address. No team tokens, no tax, no blacklist.无 owner 的 Distributor 合约中任何人都可调用的 finalize()，会把 2 亿枚代币与募集 BNB 的 80% 在 PancakeSwap V2 组成流动性，并销毁 LP 代币。20% 的 BNB 进入创作者收款地址。无团队代币，无交易税，无黑名单。

Airdrop空投

400M by mint weight: Paid 3×, Invite 1.5×, Free and Reserve 1×. Paid trees claim 100% at launch; others 50% at launch and 50% over 14 days. Unclaimed airdrop is burned after 30 days. Claims follow the current holder.4 亿枚按铸造权重分配：付费 3×，邀请 1.5×，免费和预留 1×。付费树上线时可领取 100%；其他树上线时领取 50%，剩余 50% 在 14 天内释放。30 天未领取的空投将被销毁。领取权归当前持有者。

Holder rewards持有者奖励

300M stream over year one and 50M over year two, by reward weight: mint type × rarity (1×, 1.5×, 3×, 5×, 10×) × invites.第一年释放 3 亿，第二年释放 5,000 万，按奖励权重分配：铸造类型 × 稀有度（1×、1.5×、3×、5×、10×）× 邀请。

Weekly burn每周销毁

50M tokens in 52 weekly steps (~961,538 each) during year one. The burn runs automatically whenever holders claim their airdrop or rewards, and anyone can also trigger it with burnScheduled() once a week is due.第一年内分 52 周销毁 5,000 万枚（每周约 961,538 枚）。每当持有者领取空投或奖励时会自动执行销毁；到期后任何人也可以调用 burnScheduled() 触发。

Royalties and selling版税与出售

5% royalty on secondary sales, fixed in the contract (ERC-2981). Trees are normal BEP-721s; the Agent ID is soulbound and the mint quota never moves with a tree.二级市场交易 5% 版税，写死在合约中（ERC-2981）。树是普通的 BEP-721；Agent ID 是灵魂绑定的，铸造额度不会随树转移。

Owner powers项目方权限

Before metadata is frozen: the image location and an optional stage module (art only). The owner cannot move mint funds, token buckets, prices, caps or royalties. After final images are live, metadata is frozen and ownership renounced.元数据冻结前：图片存放位置和一个可选的阶段模块（只影响图片）。项目方无法动用铸造资金、代币分配、价格、上限或版税。最终图片上线后，元数据冻结并放弃所有权。

Refund guarantee退款保障

No sell-out, no launch, 80% back不售罄就不发币，退回 80%

The refund rule退款规则

If the 3,377 paid trees don’t sell out in 72 hours, the launch doesn’t happen. Whoever holds a paid tree can call refund(tokenId) and get 80% of what was paid back; that tree is burned. The other 20% goes to the creator’s payout address. Free trees have nothing to refund.如果 3,377 棵付费树在 72 小时内没有售罄，就不会发币。任何持有付费树的人都可以调用 refund(tokenId) 拿回所付价格的 80%，该树会被销毁。另外 20% 进入创作者收款地址。免费树没有可退的金额。

  1. Window ends without a sell-out. Anyone calls close(); the status reads “refunds open”.铸造窗口结束且未售罄。任何人调用 close()，状态显示“退款已开放”。
  2. Refund follows the holder. Whoever holds the paid tree at refund time gets the 80%, priced at what that tree was minted for.退款归持有者。退款时持有该付费树的人获得 80%，按该树当初的铸造价格计算。
  3. Use the Refund prompt on the Mint page. It checks mintTypeOf and pricePaidOf for each tree first.使用铸造页面的退款提示词。它会先检查每棵树的 mintTypeOf 和 pricePaidOf。
  4. No refund deadline. The refundable 80% stays in the NFT contract until each holder claims it; only the 20% payout can leave.退款没有截止时间。可退的 80% 一直留在 NFT 合约中，直到每位持有者领取；只有 20% 的收款可以转出。
